# Learnings

## Core Rules
- Do NOT mention AI Chat Fantasy. We are not promoting. We are making people FEEL something.
- People share content when it triggers an OPINION they want to express. Comments = algorithm = views.
- Keep messages short — real humans text 3-8 words at a time. Long AI responses kill realism.
- No sugarcoating. No "I feel so much better now." That's boring. Real conversations are messier.

## The #1 Insight (discovered Feb 2026)
Every hook so far has the AI as a PASSIVE LISTENER. That's why they flop.
Viral AI content works when the AI has an OPINION that creates real-world drama.
- DEAD: "I vented to the AI and felt heard."
- ALIVE: "The AI sided with my boyfriend. I lost it."
The hook isn't "I talked to AI." The hook is "THE AI SAID SOMETHING AND NOW THERE'S DRAMA."

## What Creates Comments (the only metric that matters)
Comments happen when people have a strong opinion they MUST share.
Triggers that work:
1. AI takes a controversial side → "who's right, the AI or her?" → debate in comments
2. AI says something brutally honest → "the AI called me out" → "same fr" replies
3. Relationship jealousy angle → "boyfriend got jealous of a bot" → "red flag or nah?" debate
4. AI exposes something the user won't admit → painful truth = share-worthy

Triggers that DON'T work:
- Anything that ends with the user feeling good and resolved (no tension = no comments)
- "My friends don't understand me" → nobody cares, too passive
- "My therapist never said this" → boring, too safe
- Confession-based hooks without a real-world consequence

## Avoid These Patterns Forever
- Soft emotional validation hooks (felt seen, felt heard, felt understood)
- Hooks where nothing is at stake or at risk
- Long conversation messages — anyone who types more than 10 words is not texting, they're writing an essay
- General hooks that could apply to anyone (nobody thinks "that's me")

## What's Working
- **Relationship jealousy + AI angle (700 views, 2026-02-25)** — "boyfriend read my AI chats / the bot knows you better than I do" — HIGHEST result so far. Clear signal: jealousy hook works.
- The iMessage UI format looks credible. Format is not the problem.
- Hook framing from BF's perspective ("he got quiet") creates curiosity gap — viewer wonders what was IN the chats.

## What Flopped
- Confession hooks (0 views): too abstract, no stakes, no one to argue with
- "My sister said I'd never amount to anything" (flop): no conflict resolution, no AI opinion that divides
- Crush jealousy hook (200 views): the story is told FOR you — viewer has nothing to react to
- Short one-liner hooks with no setup: viewers don't have enough context to care
- "am I hard to love" self-doubt hook (200 views, 2026-02-26): too internal. No external person to react to, no real-world consequence. Viewer has no one to argue with in comments. Self-reflection hooks don't create debate — they create silence.

## The Toxic Friend Rule (critical — Feb 2026)
The AI must NEVER act like a therapist. Therapist AI = boring = zero shares.
Real friend AI = edge, roast, blunt truth = viral.

THERAPIST (dead):
- "what did it feel like before the performance started?"
- "that version of you is still in there. she just learned to wait."
- "some things are easier to say when there's no consequence."
→ Validating, warm, safe. Nobody shares this. Nobody comments.

REAL FRIEND (alive):
- "what did you do" (before they even finish explaining)
- "kind of." (repeating their own downplay back at them, one word)
- "so you're leaving first. genius move."
- "go text him." (doesn't let them wallow)
→ Blunt, slightly mean, uncomfortably accurate. People screenshot this.

The rule: make the user SLIGHTLY UNCOMFORTABLE, not comfortable.
Real intimacy = someone who tells you the truth with an edge.
Comfort = forgettable. Discomfort = shareable.

One-word or two-word replies from the AI hit hardest.
The AI should call out the user's own behavior, not just listen to it.
The user should get a little defensive — that's when it gets interesting.

## The Like Ratio Problem (Feb 2026)
700 views / 4 likes = 0.6% like rate. TikTok avg is ~3-5%. This means:
- People WATCHED (hook worked, format worked)
- But didn't FEEL anything by the end (conversation didn't convert emotion → action)
- The conversation was blunt but maybe too resolved. Slide 5 ended with an answer. No open loop.

**Theory:** The carousel needs to END on tension, not resolution.
- Current ending: "then at least you tried instead of punishing him..." → too tidy, feels like advice
- Better ending: leave the viewer mid-tension. "he's typing..." or cut before she texts him.
- People like when they want to know what happens next, not when it's wrapped up.

**What we need for 5K:**
1. More specific hook — "my boyfriend" is relatable but vague. Specific = credible.
   - Weak: "my boyfriend got quiet"
   - Strong: "he put his phone down and stared at me for 5 seconds"
2. Controversial AI take — the AI needs to say something that splits the comments.
   - Something half the viewers think is RIGHT and half think is WRONG.
   - "go text him" is too safe. Nobody argues with "go text him."
   - Try: AI takes the boyfriend's side. That creates fight in comments.
3. Unresolved ending — last slide must leave viewer UNSETTLED not comforted.
4. More intimate/specific detail — make viewer feel like they're watching something private.

## The Distribution Problem (March 2026) — CRITICAL
200 views / 14 likes / 4 saves on 2026-02-28 post. 7% like rate. Best content we've made. Almost nobody saw it.

**Why this happens — TikTok seed audience mechanic:**
Every post goes to a seed batch of 200-500 people first. For new/small accounts that's basically the ceiling unless the batch produces strong signals. Our 200 views IS the seed batch. The algorithm looked at signals from those 200 people and decided not to push wider.

**The gate that controls wider distribution (carousel-specific priority order):**
1. Swipe-through rate slide 1 → 2 (need 60%+ to trigger next wave) — THIS IS THE #1 KILLER
2. Dwell time per slide (3-5 seconds = reading signal)
3. Completion rate (how many reach slide 6)
4. Shares > Saves > Comments > Likes

Our 14 likes and 4 saves happened WITHIN the seed batch. But the other 186 people scrolled past slide 1 without swiping. TikTok weighs the full 200, not just the 14 who engaged. Slide 1 swipe-through rate probably killed us.

**What likely caused low distribution on 2026-02-28:**
1. Slide 1 scroll-stop not strong enough — too many people in seed batch didn't swipe in
2. Wrong posting time — seed batch served to wrong-geography users, weak first signals
3. No trending audio — missed sound-based audience injection that helps push wider
4. Account is new — smaller seed batch to begin with

**Audio matters more than we thought:**
- Using trending audio injects content into the existing audience already engaging with that sound
- No audio or generic audio = no sound-based audience boost
- Business accounts with copyrighted music get quietly throttled (no notification)
- RULE: Always use a trending sound. Find trending audio in the AI/emotional/relationship niche.

**Best posting times for Indian audience (IST):**
1. Friday/Sunday 8:00 PM – 9:30 PM IST ← BEST SLOT for emotional/companion content
2. Thursday/Friday 6:30 PM – 9:30 PM IST ← strong general window
3. Mon-Thu 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM IST ← lunch break, solid reach
4. Fri/Sat 10:00 PM – 12:00 AM IST ← late night high-intent viewers (our audience = alone at night)
- Post 30-45 min BEFORE the peak so algorithm seeds the batch before traffic spike hits
- Best days: Thursday, Friday, Sunday. Weakest: Tuesday, Saturday.

## Patterns
- The AI must DO something, not just listen. It must say something that causes a reaction.
- The reaction should be divisive — half the comments disagree, half agree.
- Relatable cringe > inspirational. People share things they're slightly embarrassed by.
- Best comment bait: anything that makes people type "but she has a point tho" or "this is toxic lmao"
- **Resolved ending = low likes.** Leave tension open. Viewer must feel unfinished.
- **Too-clean advice = safe = forgettable.** The AI should say something that makes you go "wait..."
- **#characterai on every post — non-negotiable.** It's free distribution to 20M+ pre-warmed users.
